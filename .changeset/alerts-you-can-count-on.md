fix: **Alerts you can count on.** A reply, incident or outage alert that your browser's push service couldn't take at that moment is now sent on the next check instead of being lost. Changing your alert settings while a check is running no longer gets undone, and the status pages you follow are checked in turn, however many other people follow theirs. Alerts take up to 10 status pages of your own, and a device you haven't opened PaperWing on for 60 days stops getting them (turn notifications on again to bring it back).
