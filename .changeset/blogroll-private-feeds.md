fix: **Private feeds stay private.** A feed whose address holds a password or a token, like a private GitLab or newsletter feed, is no longer published on your profile's blogroll or in a published mix and its Bluesky feed. It stays in your own copy of the mix, marked with a lock, and a mix you published before is published again without it, or unpublished if it has nothing public left. PaperWing no longer shows one from a blogroll or mix published earlier. If yours went out with a feed like that in it, its address has already been seen: reset that feed's token where you got it.
