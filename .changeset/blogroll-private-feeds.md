fix: **Private feeds stay off your public blogroll.** A feed whose address holds a password or a token, like a private GitLab or newsletter feed, is no longer published on your profile's blogroll, and PaperWing no longer shows one from a blogroll published earlier. If your blogroll was public with a feed like that in it, its address has already gone out: reset that feed's token where you got it.
